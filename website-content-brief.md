## 1. One-line pitch

**FabSuite turns a customer's window/door measurements into a costed, professional quotation and a
cutting plan — in minutes, not an evening with a calculator and Excel.**

## 2. Elevator pitch (2–3 sentences)

FabSuite is a web-based platform built specifically for aluminium and uPVC window/door fabrication shops.
A shop configures its own profile catalog, cutting formulas, and pricing once; from then on, every new
job — a window, a door, a composite frame, a folding door, a curtain-wall bay — gets accurate, live
costing and an optimized aluminium cutting plan automatically, finishing in a shop-branded PDF quotation
that looks like it came from a serious, professional operation.

## 3. Who it's for

- **Primary buyer:** owners of small-to-mid Indian aluminium/uPVC fabrication shops — currently costing
  jobs by hand, in Excel, or on paper; or using an older, expensive, inflexible desktop tool.
- Shops that fabricate: sliding windows, casement windows, tilt & turn windows, fixed lights, composite/
  multi-section windows and doors, folding (bi-fold) doors, curtain-wall/structural-glazing facades, and
  frameless glass doors/partitions.
- Teams with both an owner (who cares about margins and pricing) and shop-floor/sales staff (who need to
  build quotes and see cutting plans, but shouldn't see the shop's internal cost rates).
- Shops that currently lose money to **cutting wastage** they can't see or measure, and to **quoting
  errors** that only surface after material is bought.

## 4. The problem it solves

Fabrication shops in this space today mostly do one of three things:

1. **Manual costing in Excel or on paper** — slow, error-prone, no live recalculation when a rate
   changes, no way to optimize aluminium bar cutting to reduce wastage, and quotes that look like a
   spreadsheet, not a professional proposal.
2. **An expensive, rigid legacy desktop tool** (the category leader is EvA WinOptimize) — powerful but
   costly, desktop-only, and not built for a shop that also wants a modern sales/lead pipeline.
3. **Nothing at all for the business side** — no way to track a lead from first inquiry through site
   visit, quotation, negotiation, to a won job, so opportunities fall through the cracks.

FabSuite replaces all three: one web app, accessible from a browser on desktop or a phone on the shop
floor, that owns the whole flow from "customer walks in" to "cutting plan on the shop floor" to "PDF
quotation in the customer's inbox."

## 5. How a shop actually uses it (the core workflow)

1. **Sign up** → a 30-day free trial, no card required, the shop's own private workspace is created
   instantly.
2. **Set up once, use forever:**
   - **Profile catalog** — the aluminium/uPVC bar profiles the shop stocks (name, code, weight per
     metre).
   - **Profile systems** — the shop's own cutting formulas per window type (Sliding, Casement, Tilt &
     Turn, Fixed, Folding, Curtain Wall, Frameless…): which profiles, what basis (width/height), what
     multiplier and deduction, how many pieces per member. This is the shop's real, calibrated
     know-how, entered once.
   - **Rate card** — profile ₹/kg, powder-coating ₹/sq.ft, fabrication and installation labour rates,
     wastage %, margin %, GST, plus a flat per-project overhead if the shop charges one (e.g. delivery).
     Every edit is versioned — a rate change never rewrites a quote that already went out.
   - **Glass types, finish/colour options, and automation (motor) kits** — each with its own rate,
     entirely shop-defined.
3. **Start a project** for a customer (new or existing) and add its openings — a "window" here can be:
   - a plain single window or door (any of the shop's own systems),
   - a **composite/multi-section frame** (one outer frame, several sections side by side — e.g. a fixed
     light next to a casement, or a 3-part "Chicago" window — each section its own glass/opening type),
   - a **folding/bi-fold door** (2 to 12 leaves, correct per-junction hinge-stile and per-leaf hardware
     costing),
   - a **curtain-wall bay** (a mullion/transom grid of any size, cells individually marked vision glass,
     spandrel, openable vent, or infill panel),
   - a **frameless glass door** (glass + patch fittings, no aluminium frame to cut).
   Every opening gets a real, to-scale technical line drawing automatically — no manual sketching.
4. **Live costing, instantly** — as openings are added, FabSuite computes: profile cost (weight × rate),
   accessory/hardware cost, glazing cost, custom finish cost, motorised-unit cost, shop overhead
   (allocated fairly across every opening by area), margin, and GST — down to the paisa, with the exact
   same formula every time.
5. **See the cutting plan before you cut anything** — FabSuite bin-packs every required cut length
   against the shop's actual stock bar length (accounting for the saw's kerf and end-trim), profile code
   by profile code, and shows exactly how many bars are needed and how much gets wasted — before a single
   bar is cut, not after.
6. **Finalize the project** — a completeness check catches anything missing (a section with no glass, an
   operable sash with no hardware system, a curtain-wall cell with no role assigned) before it can be
   locked. Once finalized, the numbers are frozen forever — even if the shop's rate card changes later,
   that customer's quoted price never silently moves.
7. **Generate documents** — one click produces a shop-branded, professional **customer quotation PDF**
   (with a cover letter, per-window technical drawings, itemized pricing, terms & conditions, and a
   signature page) plus a full set of **internal reports**: cost summary, cutting schedule for the shop
   floor, a bill of quantities for procurement, and category-by-category cost breakdowns (profiles,
   accessories, glazing, finishes, overhead) — useful for the owner, hidden from staff.
8. **(Optional) Run the sales pipeline from lead to job** — capture an inquiry (phone/WhatsApp/walk-in/
   referral), log site-visit notes and measurements, track it through stages (Contacted → Site Visit →
   Quotation Sent → Negotiation → Won/Lost), get automatic reminders so a follow-up is never forgotten,
   and convert a won lead straight into a project with one click.
9. **See the business, not just the jobs** — an owner-only analytics dashboard: revenue trend, sales
   funnel, product mix (which systems/glass/finishes actually sell), margin composition, team
   performance, repeat-customer rate.

## 6. Full feature list (by module)

### Profile catalog & systems
- Full CRUD profile (bar) catalog — name, code, weight/metre.
- Profile "systems" — the shop's own cutting-formula templates per window/door type, with a live preview
  of computed cut lengths against a test size before saving.
- A calibration mode — enter a real historical job's known-correct cut lengths, and see exactly how far
  off (and in which direction) the current formula is, with the fix applied inline.
- Full accessory/hardware package per system — flat quantities, per-sq.ft quantities, per-leaf quantities
  (for folding doors), category (hardware vs. accessory), unit, brand/colour/product-name labels for the
  customer-facing quote.
- Brand, series, sightline, and a performance spec block (U-value, acoustic rating, wind load, air/water
  class, warranty) per system — prints on the customer quotation when set.

### Window & opening designer
- Plain windows/doors, composite/multi-section frames, folding doors, curtain-wall bays, and frameless
  glass — all in one designer, all costed and cut-planned through the same engine.
- A real, to-scale technical drawing generated automatically for every opening (sliding, casement,
  tilt & turn, folding zig-zag, curtain-wall grid with role-shaded cells, frameless glass with
  patch-fitting marks) — matching the drawing conventions the industry's own reference software uses.
- Duplicate an opening (or a whole project) as a starting point for a similar job.

### Costing engine
- Profile cost, accessory/hardware cost, glazing cost, custom finish cost (single or dual
  outside/inside finish), motorised-automation cost — all computed live, to the paisa.
- Shop overhead (powder-coating, fabrication labour, installation labour, wastage allowance, and an
  optional flat per-project charge) allocated fairly across every opening by its share of total area.
- Margin and GST (CGST + SGST) applied on top, with the tax split shown separately on the quote.
- A project once finalized is frozen — later rate-card changes never retroactively alter a quote that
  already went to a customer.

### Cutting optimization
- Bin-packing (First-Fit-Decreasing) against the shop's real stock bar length, honoring the saw's kerf
  loss and end-trim allowance.
- Grouped by profile code across the *entire* project, not window-by-window — the real efficiency gain
  comes from planning cuts across every opening at once.
- A clear wastage % per profile and for the whole project, so a shop can see exactly what a job costs in
  material efficiency, not just ₹.
- Anything too long to fit any bar is flagged clearly, never silently mis-packed.

### Quotation & reports (PDF)
- A shop-branded, professional customer quotation: logo, letterhead, cover letter, per-window technical
  drawing + itemized spec, a quote-total summary page, terms & conditions, and a signature page.
- Internal-only reports (kept away from staff who shouldn't see shop margins): cost summary, cutting
  schedule, bill of quantities, and category cost-breakdown reports (profiles/accessories/glazing/
  finishes/overhead).
- A finalized project's PDF is generated once and served identically forever; a draft project's PDF is
  always live and clearly marked DRAFT/ESTIMATE.

### Projects & customers
- First-class customer records (linked or created inline while starting a project).
- Draft → Finalized project lifecycle; a finalized project is read-only, its numbers permanently locked.
- Duplicate a finished project as a fresh draft for a similar customer/job.
- Search and filter the project list; live totals shown without waiting for a page reload.

### Sales pipeline (leads)
- Capture a lead with just a name, phone, and source (phone inquiry, WhatsApp, walk-in, referral —
  customer/architect/contractor, etc.).
- Duplicate-phone detection against existing leads and customers.
- A clear stage pipeline: New Inquiry → Contacted → Site Visit Scheduled → Site Visit Done → Quotation
  Sent → Negotiation → Won/Lost (with a required reason when marked lost).
- An activity log per lead (calls, visits, notes) — including structured site-visit measurements.
- Automatic follow-up reminders, including a two-touch schedule (a midpoint check-in, then a final
  reminder) once a quote is sent, tied to the shop's own quote-validity period.
- A "My Follow-ups" view (overdue / due today / due this week) and a dashboard widget so nothing slips.
- One-click conversion of a won lead into a real project, keeping the customer's details and the lead's
  history linked together.
- Lead reports: conversion by source, average days to win, lost-reason breakdown, per-salesperson
  performance — owner-only.

### Analytics dashboard (owner-only)
- Revenue trend, sales funnel, product mix (which systems/glass/finishes/opening types actually sell),
  cost composition, team performance, top and repeat customers — all computed from real finalized-project
  data, not estimates.

### Team & roles
- Two roles: **Owner** (full access — rates, margins, team, billing, internal cost reports) and
  **Staff** (can build and manage projects/leads/quotes, but never sees the shop's internal cost rates,
  margins, or billing).
- Invite teammates by email; deactivate an account without losing its history.
- Every login, failed login, and sensitive action is audit-logged.

### Billing
- One simple plan: **₹999/month**, flat, full-featured — no tiers, no hidden limits.
- **30-day free trial**, no credit card required to start.
- Billing handled securely through Razorpay; invoices available any time.

### Trust & security
- Every shop's data lives in its own fully isolated workspace — no cross-shop visibility, ever.
- Server-side validation and authorization on every action, not just hidden buttons.
- Every sensitive action (rate changes, team changes, deactivations, billing events) is logged.
- Built as a modern, responsive web app — works on a desktop in the office and a phone on the shop floor.

## 7. What makes FabSuite different (positioning angles)

- **Built for the full range of what a real fabrication shop actually sells** — not just plain sliding
  and casement windows, but composite/multi-section frames, folding doors, curtain-wall facades, and
  frameless glass too, all costed and cut-planned through the same one engine, not bolted-on afterthoughts.
- **The shop's own formulas, not a fixed template.** Every cutting formula, every accessory, every rate is
  entered and owned by the shop — FabSuite doesn't assume how a shop cuts; it captures how *this* shop
  cuts.
- **A quote that looks like it came from a serious operation.** The customer-facing PDF format was built
  to match the output of the industry's own established reference tool, field-by-field — a shop's
  customers see the same professional standard they'd expect from an established competitor, without the
  established competitor's price tag or rigidity.
- **The whole business, not just the drawing board.** Most costing tools stop at the quote. FabSuite also
  runs the sales pipeline that got the customer to the quote, and the analytics that show whether the
  business is actually working.
- **Nothing gets forgotten.** Automatic follow-up reminders, a completeness check before anything can be
  finalized, and frozen numbers on every finalized quote mean nothing slips through a crack that used to
  cost real money.
- **Modern and accessible.** A real web app — no installation, works from a phone on the shop floor,
  always the latest version, nothing to maintain.
- **Honest pricing.** One plan, one price, a real 30-day trial with no card required — not a maze of
  tiers designed to upsell later.

## 10. Tone & voice guidance

- Speak to a **shop owner**, not a developer — plain, concrete, benefit-first language. "See your cutting
  wastage before you cut" beats "bin-packing algorithm."
- Respect the audience: experienced tradespeople, price-sensitive, not impressed by jargon. Avoid generic
  SaaS clichés ("revolutionize," "seamless," "empower").
- Every claim on the page should be something the product genuinely does today — nothing aspirational or
  roadmap-only should be presented as current.
- Keep the visual language aligned with the product's own: a warm ink/charcoal + burnt-orange accent
  palette (`#1B2430` ink, `#B5651D` accent) reads as professional and trade-appropriate, not generic tech
  startup.
