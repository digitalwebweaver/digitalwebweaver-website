## FAQ Section
1. **Custom build vs WooCommerce?** — WooCommerce for quick launch with plugin ecosystem. Custom for unique checkout flows, complex integrations, or performance requirements exceeding WooCommerce limits.
2. **What is headless commerce?** — Next.js frontend consuming WooCommerce or Shopify via API. Faster loads, unlimited design freedom, and better mobile UX.
3. **How do you handle high traffic events?** — Auto-scaling infra, CDN for static assets, Redis caching, and pre-emptive load testing before peak events.
4. **Can you integrate with our warehouse system?** — Yes — real-time stock sync with 3PL providers, warehouse management systems, and ERP via REST or EDI.
