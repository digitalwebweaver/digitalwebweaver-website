## Core Features

1. **Inbound Call Handling** — Answers every incoming call instantly, any time of day, in a natural voice trained on your business information.
2. **Lead Qualification & Routing** — Asks the right questions to qualify callers and routes serious prospects to your team with full call context.
3. **Appointment Booking** — Books and confirms appointments directly into your calendar without any manual coordination.
4. **Outbound Follow-up Calls** — Places follow-up calls to leads and customers — checking in, confirming details, or re-engaging cold enquiries.
5. **Reminder Calls** — Automated reminder calls for appointments, payments, or renewals — reducing no-shows and missed deadlines.
6. **CRM & Calendar Integration** — Syncs call outcomes, bookings, and lead data directly with your CRM or scheduling system in real time.

## FAQs
1. **Will callers know they're speaking to an AI?** The agent is trained to sound natural and conversational using your business's tone and information — most callers experience it as a smooth, helpful conversation.
2. **What happens with calls it can't fully handle?** Complex or sensitive calls are transferred to your team with full context, so the caller never has to repeat themselves from scratch.
3. **Can it make outbound calls too, or only answer inbound?** Both — it can handle inbound enquiries and place outbound follow-up, reminder, or re-engagement calls.
4. **Does it work in the languages our customers use?** Yes — the agent can be configured for multiple languages based on your customer base.
5. **How long before it's live and answering our calls?** Most businesses are live within 3–4 weeks, including training the agent on your information and connecting it to your systems.
