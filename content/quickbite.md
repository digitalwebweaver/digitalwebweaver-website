## Core Features: "What's Inside"

1. **Customer Ordering App** — Branded iOS and Android apps where customers browse menus, customise orders, pay, and track delivery live.
2. **Restaurant Dashboard** — Manage incoming orders, build and update menus, track sales, and view performance analytics from one screen.
3. **Delivery Partner App** — Drivers receive order pickups, get optimised navigation, and track their earnings — all from a dedicated app.
4. **Real-Time Order Tracking** — Live GPS tracking with accurate ETAs keeps customers informed from kitchen to doorstep.
5. **Payments & Cash-on-Delivery** — UPI, cards, net-banking, and COD supported out of the box, with automatic reconciliation and payouts.
6. **Offers, Coupons & Loyalty** — Run discount codes, first-order offers, and repeat-customer loyalty programs to grow direct orders.

## FAQs

1. **Do we still need to be listed on aggregator apps too?** — That's entirely your call. Many of our clients keep aggregator listings for discovery while actively pushing repeat customers to their own branded app to avoid commissions on those orders.
2. **How do we get our delivery riders using the app?** — We help you onboard your existing riders onto the driver app as part of launch — most teams adapt within a few days.
3. **Can we run multiple restaurant brands from one kitchen?** — Yes — cloud kitchen operators can run several branded ordering apps and menus from a single restaurant dashboard.
4. **What payment methods are supported?** — UPI, debit/credit cards, net-banking, popular wallets, and cash-on-delivery — all with automatic order reconciliation.
5. **How long before we can start taking orders through our own app?** — Most restaurants go live within 6–8 weeks, including branding, menu setup, app store approval, and rider onboarding.
