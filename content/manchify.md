## Core Features (10 Modules)

1. **Leads & CRM** — Capture enquiries from web forms and WhatsApp, work a visual pipeline, and never drop a follow-up — all in one inbox.
2. **Quotes & GST Invoicing** — Branded quotes with live margins, e-sign approval, and one-click conversion to GST-correct invoices with CGST/SGST/IGST split.
3. **Events & Run-Sheets** — A dedicated workspace per event — timeline, tasks, team assignments, and documents — so nothing slips on the day.
4. **Vendors & Purchase Orders** — Issue POs, maintain a settlement ledger, and get automatic RCM flagging for unregistered vendors so you never lose ITC.
5. **Manpower & Attendance** — Plan headcount, deploy staff, mark mobile attendance offline, and compute wages — no paper, no disputes.
6. **Rentals & Inventory** — Availability-aware bookings, dispatch and return tracking, and damage recording — no double-booking ever again.
7. **Money, P&L & GST Centre** — Per-event profit tracking, collection milestones, payment links, and a GST centre with GSTR-ready exports.
8. **Reports & Analytics** — A live business dashboard showing revenue trends, pipeline health, and vendor spend — permission-scoped for your team.
9. **Client & Field Portals** — A branded portal for clients to pay and approve; a mobile PWA for your ground teams working offline.
10. **WhatsApp-Native Integration** — Quotes, invoices, and payment reminders go out on WhatsApp — the channel your clients and vendors actually use.

## How It Works (3-Step Process)

1. **Capture & Quote** — Leads flow in from your site and WhatsApp. Build a branded GST-correct quote in under 60 seconds and send it for e-sign approval.
2. **Plan & Run** — On approval, the quote becomes a live event workspace — vendors, manpower, rentals, run-sheet, and tasks connected in one view.
3. **Bill & Get Paid** — Generate a GST invoice, send a payment link, chase milestones automatically, and see your per-event P&L the moment costs land.

## FAQs

1. **Can I run multiple event types in one account?** — Yes. Manchify supports six business presets and you can toggle the exact modules each team or event type needs.
2. **Does it handle Indian GST correctly?** — Yes, and this is a core differentiator. Manchify handles CGST/SGST vs IGST routing automatically, flags unregistered vendor invoices for RCM, tracks ITC-at-risk amounts, and produces GSTR-ready export files.
3. **What if my team has no tech experience?** — Manchify is no-code and mobile-first. Setup takes under 10 minutes. Your team marks attendance on their phone, your managers see P&L on a dashboard.
4. **How is it different from a spreadsheet or generic CRM?** — A generic CRM handles leads; Manchify handles leads, quotes, vendors, manpower, rentals, invoicing, and GST in one connected system. No data re-entry between stages.
