## Core Features: "What's Inside"

1. **Rider App** — Riders book instantly or schedule rides, see live fare estimates, track drivers in real time, and pay in-app.
2. **Driver App** — Drivers receive ride requests, get optimised navigation, manage availability, and track daily earnings.
3. **Dispatch Dashboard** — Operators monitor live rides, manage driver onboarding, assign rides manually when needed, and view fleet analytics.
4. **Live GPS Tracking** — Real-time location sharing between rider and driver with accurate ETAs throughout the trip.
5. **Smart Fare Estimation** — Distance, time, and demand-based fare calculation shown upfront — fully configurable to your pricing model.
6. **Payments & Driver Payouts** — UPI, cards, wallets, and cash supported, with automatic fare splits and scheduled driver payouts.

## Target Audience / "Built For"

3. **Does it support both instant and scheduled bookings?** — Yes — instant bookings and advance scheduling, plus recurring corporate pickups supported.
4. **Can dispatch staff manually assign rides when needed?** — Yes — dispatch dashboard allows live ride monitoring and manual assignment/reassignment capabilities.
