## Process Section — "From demo to live in under 4 weeks"
1. **Pick your product — 1 day** — Schedule a 30-minute demo. We walk through the product, identify customisations, and give you a fixed price and timeline.
2. **Customisation sprint — 1–2 weeks** — We brand it, configure your modules, wire in your integrations, and load your seed data. You get daily progress updates.
3. **UAT and training — 3–5 days** — Your team tests everything on a staging environment. We run a training session and fix any outstanding issues.
4. **Launch and handover — day 1** — We deploy to production, configure your domain, and hand over the full source code. You own it outright — no licence fees, ever.
