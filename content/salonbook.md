## Core Features

1. **Online Booking & Scheduling** — Clients book services online with real-time stylist and chair availability — no phone tag required.
2. **Staff Scheduling & Commission** — Manage stylist shifts, service assignments, and automatic commission calculations on every booking.
3. **Billing, Packages & Memberships** — Sell service packages, prepaid memberships, and gift cards with integrated billing and balance tracking.
4. **Inventory & Retail Sales** — Track product stock for in-salon use and retail sales, with automatic deduction and low-stock alerts.
5. **Loyalty & Referral Programs** — Run point-based loyalty programs and referral rewards that bring clients back.
6. **Automated Reminders & Rebooking** — Cut no-shows with appointment reminders, and prompt clients to rebook automatically.

## Target Audience / Use Cases

- **Hair & beauty salons** — Manage stylist-wise bookings, service menus, and retail product sales.
