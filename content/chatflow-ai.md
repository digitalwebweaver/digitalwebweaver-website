## Core Features

1. **Instant, Natural Replies** — Responds to enquiries immediately in natural language, trained on your products, services, and FAQs.
2. **Lead Qualification & Routing** — Asks the right questions to qualify leads and routes hot prospects to your sales team with full context.
3. **Order & Booking Updates** — Sends automatic order confirmations, status updates, and appointment reminders without manual effort.
4. **Catalogue & In-Chat Payments** — Shares product catalogues and accepts payments directly within the WhatsApp conversation.
5. **Broadcast & Re-engagement Campaigns** — Send promotions, restock alerts, and win-back messages to segmented customer lists — compliantly.
6. **CRM & Order System Integration** — Syncs conversations and customer data with your CRM, order system, or booking platform in real time.
