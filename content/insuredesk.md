## Core Features ("What's Inside")

1. **Lead Management** — Capture, qualify, and assign leads from multiple sources, with follow-up tracking through to conversion.
2. **Policy Tracking** — Maintain a complete record of every client's policies — type, premium, term, and status — in one place.
3. **Automated Renewal Reminders** — SMS/WhatsApp/email reminders go out automatically ahead of renewal dates.
4. **Claims Follow-up** — Track claim status from filing to settlement, with reminders.
5. **Commission Tracking** — Track commissions earned per policy and agent, with clear reporting.
6. **Client Portal & Document Vault** — Give clients a branded portal to view policies, download documents.
