## Core Features

1. **Job Scheduling & Dispatch** — Create, assign, and track service jobs with technician calendars and real-time status updates.
2. **Technician Routing** — Optimize daily routes for field technicians and track job progress via a dedicated mobile app.
3. **Service History & Reports** — Maintain complete treatment history per property with digital service reports accessible to clients.
4. **AMC & Contract Management** — Track annual maintenance contracts with automatic visit scheduling and renewal alerts.
5. **Quotes, Billing & Invoicing** — Generate quotes and invoices on-site, collect digital payments, and track outstanding dues.
6. **Automated Client Reminders** — SMS/WhatsApp notifications for upcoming visits, contract renewals, and post-service feedback.
