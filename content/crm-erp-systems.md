## FAQ — CRM/ERP Development FAQ
1. **Custom vs off-the-shelf — when to go custom?** — When your workflows are non-standard, integration requirements are complex, or Salesforce/SAP license costs exceed custom build cost.
2. **Can you integrate our existing systems?** — Yes — legacy databases, third-party APIs, and file-based EDI integrations are all part of our CRM/ERP delivery.
3. **How long does a CRM build take?** — Core CRM with contacts, pipeline, and reporting: 8–12 weeks. Full ERP with multiple modules: 4–9 months.
4. **Can users configure workflows themselves?** — Yes — we build workflow configuration UIs so business users can modify approval chains and automation rules without developer involvement.
