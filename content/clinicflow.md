## Core Features

1. **Appointment Scheduling** — Online and walk-in booking with doctor-wise calendars, automated reminders, and waitlist management.
2. **Electronic Medical Records** — Structured patient histories, prescriptions, vitals, and visit notes accessible securely across visits and doctors.
3. **Billing & Insurance Claims** — Generate invoices, track payments, apply package pricing, and manage insurance claim documentation.
4. **Pharmacy & Inventory** — Manage in-house pharmacy stock, dispense against prescriptions, and get low-stock alerts automatically.
5. **Lab Order Integration** — Order tests, track results, and attach reports directly to a patient's record for the consulting doctor.
6. **Patient App & Reminders** — Patients book appointments, view prescriptions and reports, and get visit reminders from a branded app.
