# Privacy Policy (`/privacy-policy/`)

> Note: Site-wide header/footer nav already captured in `content/home.md` — not repeated in full here. Long boilerplate paragraphs summarized below; all section headings and key terms preserved.

## 1. Overview
Digital Web Weaver commits to protecting privacy for clients, website visitors, and prospects. The policy covers information collected via digitalwebweaver.com and related services. Users agree to these terms by using the website; disagreement means non-use.

## 2. What We Collect

**Directly provided information:**
- Name, job title, email, phone number
- Company details (name, size, industry)
- Project details and communications
- Payment data (processed by third parties; card details not stored)

**Automatically collected data:**
- IP address and approximate location (country/city level)
- Browser/device/OS information
- Page visit patterns, referral sources, click behavior, scroll depth

**Third-party sources:**
- LinkedIn, Clutch, referral partners, professional directories

## 3. How We Use It

**Processing purposes:**
- Service delivery
- Communication and support
- Marketing (newsletters, case studies — opt-out available)
- Analytics and site improvement
- Legal compliance (accounting, tax, data protection)
- Security and fraud prevention

**Legal bases:** Contract performance, legitimate interests, legal obligation, consent

## 4. Sharing Data

No selling of personal data. Limited sharing with:
- Service providers: AWS, Google Analytics, HubSpot, Stripe, Mailchimp
- Business transfers (with advance notice if acquisition/merger occurs)
- Legal requirements or court orders
- Protection of rights/safety

**Cross-border transfer:** Data not transferred outside the EEA without safeguards like Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. Cookies

**Types used:**
- Essential (required; non-negotiable)
- Analytics (Google Analytics; consent-based)
- Marketing (retargeting; consent-based)

Managed via cookie banner or browser settings. Disabling some cookies may degrade site experience.

## 6. Data Retention

| Data Type | Retention Period |
|-----------|------------------|
| Client records | 7 years post-project |
| Prospective inquiries | 2 years from last contact |
| Newsletter subscribers | Until unsubscribe |
| Analytics | 26 months |
| Security logs | 12 months |

After expiration: data is deleted or anonymized.

## 7. Your Rights

**Available rights (location-dependent):**
- Access to held data
- Correction of inaccurate information
- Erasure ("right to be forgotten")
- Processing restriction
- Data portability
- Objection to processing
- Consent withdrawal

**Response timeframe:** 30 days
**Contact:** info@digitalwebweaver.com
**EEA residents:** May lodge complaints with national data protection authority

## 8. Security

**Measures implemented:**
- TLS 1.3 encryption in transit; AES-256 at rest
- Role-based access control
- Regular security audits
- Employee training
- ISO 27001-aligned incident response

**Limitation stated:** No system is completely secure. Report suspected breaches to info@digitalwebweaver.com immediately.

## 9. Children
Services target business professionals and are not directed at under-16 users. Inadvertent collection of child data triggers immediate deletion.

## 10. Changes to This Policy
Updates posted with revised "Last updated" date. Material changes receive prominent notice (email/banner). Continued use constitutes acceptance.
