Why choose Flutter for an app?

Flutter builds iOS and Android apps from one Dart codebase with its own rendering engine, so both apps look and behave the same and run smoothly. One codebase usually means a lower cost and faster updates than two native apps.

Can you also build the backend?

Yes. We build the APIs, admin panels and databases behind the app in Laravel, Node.js or Python, or use Firebase where it fits, so one team owns the whole product.

Flutter or React Native?

Both are good. Flutter suits apps with custom, animated interfaces and a consistent look across platforms; React Native suits teams already using React on the web. We build in both and recommend one after hearing your project.

Do you publish the app to the stores?

Yes. We prepare the store listings and submit to the Google Play Store and Apple App Store, and handle review feedback until the app is live.

How do I hire a Flutter developer from you?

Choose a fixed-price project, a dedicated Flutter developer billed monthly, or a support retainer. Tell us about your app and we share profiles or an itemised estimate within 48 hours.

Who owns the code?

You do. You get the full source code, design files and store accounts in your name at handover.
