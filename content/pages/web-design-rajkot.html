Yes. Custom ERP is one of our most common projects. We build systems covering production planning, raw material inventory, job cards, quality checks, dispatch, and billing. A custom ERP costs significantly less than SAP or Tally Enterprise and is built exactly for your processes.

Yes. A B2B dealer portal lets your distributors and dealers place orders online 24/7 — with your product catalogue, their credit limit, real-time stock visibility, and automatic GST invoicing. This replaces phone/WhatsApp ordering and reduces order errors significantly.
