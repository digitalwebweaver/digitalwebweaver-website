Do I need a technical background to partner with you?

No. You bring the client and the relationship; we handle requirements, design, development, testing, hosting and support. If you can explain what a business needs, we can turn it into a scope and a price you can present.

Do I have to pay anything upfront?

No. There is no joining fee and no deposit to start. Depending on the project, we can start without an advance from your client too. We agree the terms in writing before work begins, so you know exactly what you owe and when.

What if my client doesn't pay?

You pay us only when your client pays you. If a client stops paying, you don't carry the loss on our side of the work. That is the core of the model: no risk on payment for the partner.

Can I sell your work under my own brand?

Yes. On white-label projects your client sees only your company: your name on proposals, your logo on the app, your domain and your email. We stay in the background and never contact your client directly unless you ask us to.

How much can I earn?

You set the price for your client. Our price to you is fixed and agreed per project, and the difference is yours. Partners who already have an audience or client base, such as marketers, consultants and influencers, usually earn the most.

What support do I get?

A named contact on call and WhatsApp, help preparing proposals and estimates, joining your client calls when you want a technical voice, demos of our ready-made products, and regular progress updates you can forward to your client.

What kind of projects can I sell?

Websites, web apps, mobile apps, SaaS products, CRMs and ERPs, AI automation, and our ready-made products for clinics, salons, real estate, schools, societies, solar installers and factories, which can be rebranded and launched in weeks.
