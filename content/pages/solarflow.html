Is my data separate from other companies?

Yes — hard boundary. SolarFlow is multi-tenant with per-company database scoping and Postgres Row-Level Security. One company can never see another's data.

Does it work for my DISCOM (MGVCL / DGVCL / PGVCL)?

The Net-Meter Pre-Check and subsidy lifecycle are built specifically for Gujarat DISCOMs. MGVCL is fully mapped; DGVCL and PGVCL rejection patterns are added as part of onboarding.

Can I use my own logo and branding?

Yes — white-label per company. Quotation PDFs and customer-facing documents carry your brand, not ours.

Does it handle GST correctly?

Yes. Invoices automatically apply CGST+SGST for intra-state transactions and IGST for inter-state. B2B invoices include the buyer's GSTIN for their Input Tax Credit claim.

Do field technicians need a laptop?

No. The field app is mobile-first — survey capture, site photos, and job completion on any Android or iOS phone. Works on 4G or offline.

Which AI provider does it use?

Your choice — Claude (Anthropic) or OpenAI, switchable per company with your own API key. Nothing reaches a customer without your review.

What does 'ALMM validation' mean?

ALMM (Approved List of Models and Manufacturers) is the MNRE list of subsidy-eligible panels and inverters. Using a non-ALMM item forfeits the customer's subsidy. SolarFlow flags any off-list item before you order.
