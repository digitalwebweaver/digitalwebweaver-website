What is Nivas?

Nivas is housing society management software. It handles visitor approvals, maintenance bills, UPI payments, notices, polls and complaints in one app.

Who uses Nivas?

The society committee, residents and guards. Each has its own role in the same app.

How does visitor approval work in Nivas?

The resident approves the visitor on their phone, and the guard checks the visitor in once that approval is given.

How do residents pay maintenance bills?

The committee raises maintenance bills for every flat from one place, and residents pay by UPI.

Which languages does Nivas support?

Gujarati, Hindi and English.
