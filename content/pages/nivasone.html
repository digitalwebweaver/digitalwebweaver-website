What is NivasGate?

NivasGate is society management software for Indian housing societies, built by Digital Web Weaver. It covers visitor approval at the gate, owner and tenant records, maintenance billing with UPI, society accounts, notices, polls, complaints and SOS, in Gujarati, Hindi and English.

How much does NivasGate cost?

One plan for every society: ₹10 per flat per month plus GST, with no limit on flats, residents or gate devices, and a 30-day free trial. There are no ads and no paid resident tier.

How does visitor approval work?

The guard picks the visitor type and flat, takes a photo and sends a request to the resident's phone. If nobody answers, it escalates to WhatsApp and then a phone call. A visitor is never approved automatically.

Where does maintenance money go?

Into the society's own payment-gateway account, settling to its own bank. Residents can pay by UPI, net banking or card, upload proof of a cheque or transfer, or set up UPI AutoPay.

Which languages does NivasGate support?

Gujarati, Hindi and English. Each person can switch language at any time.
