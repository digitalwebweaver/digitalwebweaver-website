What we fix in a factory

  • Slow, inconsistent quotes

    Rates drift from person to person and every quote is retyped into Word.

  • Costly changeovers

    Nobody can see which pending orders share a specification and could run together.

  • Material surprises

    A run starts and then stops because a raw material is short.

  • QC disconnected from dispatch

    Goods leave the gate without a recorded quality check.

  • Billing leaks

    Invoices retyped from challans, GST split by hand, delivered orders never invoiced.

  • ERPs that don't fit

    Generic software doesn't understand your product, and big ERPs take months and cost too much.

What manufacturing software do you build?

Manufacturing ERPs, production planning and scheduling, inventory and raw-material tracking, quality control, dispatch and GST invoicing, dealer and distributor portals, and shop-floor apps. We build custom systems and also offer ready-made products for panel, fabrication and chemical businesses.

Have you built software for real factories?

Yes. PanelOS, our ERP for PUF and PIR sandwich panel factories, is in daily production use at a panel manufacturer. It runs from enquiry and BOQ to production planning, QC, dispatch and GST invoicing. FabSuite and ChemSupply serve fabricators and chemical suppliers.

Can it work with Tally?

Yes. Our manufacturing systems export sales, GST registers and ledgers to Tally in XML or CSV, so your accountant keeps working the way they do today.

Why not use a big ERP like SAP?

Big ERPs are expensive, take months to set up and rarely understand your product, such as square-metre pricing with cut lengths or batch and expiry rules. Software built around your process gets used on the shop floor, and you own it.

How long does a manufacturing ERP take?

A ready-made product can go live in days to weeks. A custom ERP usually starts with a first module, such as quotations and orders, live in 6 to 10 weeks, with production, inventory and billing added in phases.

Does it work on the shop floor?

Yes. Supervisors and dispatch staff use phone and tablet screens or an Android app to update production stages with photos, record QC and close dispatches with proof of delivery.
