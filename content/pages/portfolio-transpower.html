TPE Transpower India Pvt Ltd has made transformers, inductors, detuned reactors and other magnetics in Vadodara's Makarpura GIDC since 2002, for clients across India and abroad. We designed and built its company website on WordPress: a custom design with a full product catalogue and a quote form on every product page.

In short

Digital Web Weaver designed and built transpowerelectronics.in, the company website of TPE Transpower India Pvt Ltd, a Vadodara manufacturer of transformers, inductors and detuned reactors founded in 2002. The site runs on WordPress with a custom design, and has 20+ product pages, an applications section, clientele, downloads and a reCAPTCHA-protected quote form plus WhatsApp chat on every product page, so buyers can ask for a price from the product they are reading about.

What the website had to do

  • Show the full range

    Transformers, inductors, detuned reactors, chokes, filters, CTs and PTs, solar and special-application products, each with its own page.

  • Turn readers into enquiries

    Industrial buyers want a price for a specific product, so the quote form had to sit beside every product, not on a separate contact page.

  • Build trust with big buyers

    Show the factory, the quality policy, certifications and the companies Transpower already supplies.

  • Be found for each product

    Buyers search by product and "manufacturer in India", so every product needed a page written for that search.

  • Stay easy to update

    The team needed to add products and upload catalogues themselves.

// what we built — WordPress, custom design

A product-led website for a manufacturer

01 · Homepage

The story, the factory and a quote form above the fold

A full-width product slider opens the site, followed by the company story, four promises (design, performance, quality checks, timely delivery) and a "Request a quote" form beside them.

Further down, a section with the Makarpura factory photo introduces the company, followed by the quality-control policy, mission and vision. On a phone the layout stacks into one column with WhatsApp one tap away.

  • Quote form on the first screen
  • Factory photo for trust

02 · Products

A page for every product, each with its own quote form

More than 20 product pages cover cast resin, distribution, solar, control and isolation transformers, detuned and air-core reactors, line and load chokes, DC chokes, sine-wave and dv/dt filters, passive harmonic filters, current and potential transformers, APFC panels and more.

Each page has a product photo, a heading written the way buyers search, such as "Detuned Reactor Manufacturer in India", the technical description and a reCAPTCHA-protected quote form alongside.

  • Heading per product search
  • Quote form beside every product
  • WhatsApp chat on every page

03 · Trust

Applications and the clients who already buy

An applications section shows where the products are used, from CNC machines, control panels, UPS and lighting to filters, telecom, packaging and SPM machines, power-factor improvement, PLCs and thyristor drives.

A clientele carousel shows the industrial companies Transpower supplies, the strongest signal for a new buyer comparing manufacturers.

What did Digital Web Weaver build for Transpower?

The company website for TPE Transpower India Pvt Ltd, a Vadodara manufacturer of transformers, inductors and detuned reactors: a custom design on WordPress with 20+ product pages, an applications section, clientele, downloads, two factory addresses and a quote form on every product page.

Why WordPress for a manufacturer's website?

The Transpower team can add products, edit specifications and upload catalogues themselves without a developer, while the custom design keeps the site looking like an established engineering company rather than a template.

How does the site generate enquiries?

Every product page carries a "Request a quote" form beside the content, protected by reCAPTCHA v3, plus a WhatsApp chat button, so a buyer can ask for a price from the exact product they are reading about.

Is it built for search?

Each product has its own page targeting the way buyers search, for example "Detuned Reactor Manufacturer in India", with a clear heading, a product photo, a technical description and the quote form.
