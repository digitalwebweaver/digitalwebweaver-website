NivasGate: society software from the gate to the accounts

Most Indian housing societies run on a visitor register, a WhatsApp group, an Excel sheet and a notebook. NivasGate replaces all four with one product: a guard tablet app, a phone-first resident app and a full web dashboard for the committee, in Gujarati, Hindi and English. The first society using it is in Vadodara.

In short

NivasGate is a multi-society SaaS for Indian housing societies, designed and built by Digital Web Weaver and live at nivasgate.com. // the problem — a register, a WhatsApp group and a notebook

What societies were living with

  • Visitors let in by habit

    A guard rings a flat, nobody answers, and the visitor walks in anyway. The register can't say who entered last Tuesday.

  • Nobody sure who lives where

    Owner or tenant, since when, whether police verification was done, whether the agreement has expired.

  • Maintenance chased by hand

    Bills go out late, reminders depend on one person's memory, part payments get lost, and the treasurer rebuilds the books at year end.

  • Decisions lost in chat

    Notices, complaints and approvals drown in WhatsApp groups, with no record of who decided what.

  • Apps that sell to residents

    The big apps push ads and charge residents for a premium tier, and most are English-first though guards and many residents aren't.

// what we built — nothing approved automatically

One product, from the gate to the accounts

Screenshots show a demo society, "Sample Heights". All residents, vendors, amounts and messages in them are made up.

01 · Gate

A guard app that needs no training

Pick the visitor type, pick the flat, take a photo, send. The guard app runs full-screen on a tablet, signs in with a PIN and keeps working when the network drops.

The resident gets a request with a chime; if nobody answers it escalates to WhatsApp, then a phone call, and the guard sees every step. Residents can send guests a 6-digit code or QR in advance, daily help get their own passcodes, and parcels are handed over only against a 4-digit OTP.

  • No auto-approve, ever
  • Invites, passcodes and parcel OTPs
  • Entry photos kept for 90 days

02 · Homes & people

Every flat, owner and tenant on record

An occupancy map shows every tower, floor and flat coloured by owner, tenant, vacant, locked or under renovation. Residents and tenancies carry agreement dates, police-verification status and expiry alerts, with contact and ID details masked unless a role may see them.

Flats are set up in minutes or imported from a spreadsheet, documents are kept per flat with a verify step, and move-in and move-out requests go on a weekly calendar so a move never blocks the service lift by surprise.

03 · Billing & collection

Maintenance that collects itself

Charge heads by area or per flat, a monthly bill run with a preview, and bill and receipt numbers that never skip. Dues are shown by age, and a reminder ladder runs by itself on WhatsApp, SMS or email, skipping flats that have paid.

Every bill carries a link to a public pay page with UPI, net banking and card, paid into the society's own gateway account. Residents can upload proof of a cheque or transfer, or approve UPI AutoPay once. Payment plans, committee-approved waivers, instant no-dues certificates and bank reconciliation are built in.

  • Money never passes through us
  • UPI AutoPay with notice before each debit
  • Bank and gateway reconciliation

04 · Accounts

A real double-entry ledger from day one

Everything is posted to a double-entry ledger, so the dashboard, the bills and the statements always agree. A chart of accounts with society heads built in, numbered expense vouchers that wait for committee approval above a limit, vendor payments with TDS, budget against actual, and contract renewals.

Trial balance, income and expenditure, balance sheet, receipts and payments, day book and cash and bank book come out of the books directly, with a year-closing check and a Tally export for the accountant.

  • Vendor PAN and bank details encrypted
  • Thirteen exportable reports
  • Tally export

05 · Community & safety

Notices, polls, complaints and SOS

Notices go to the app and to WhatsApp or email and show how many people each reached. Polls count one vote per flat or per person and publish a result. Complaints move across a board with response times and automatic escalation.

A resident holds the SOS button for two seconds and picks medical, fire, security or lift stuck; the guards and committee are alerted with the flat and time, and the guard's screen turns red until someone acknowledges it.

06 · Phones

The committee doesn't need a laptop

Below 1024 pixels every page switches to an app layout with a bottom tab bar and tap targets of at least 44 pixels. On each release an automated check visits every page at desktop and phone widths and fails if anything scrolls sideways. Residents can raise the text size, and switch between Gujarati, Hindi and English at any time.

07 · Front door

A website that signs societies up

The public site explains the product, shows it working on mock devices and lets a society register itself in five steps: details, blocks and flats, documents, plan and consent. The platform team reviews the request and approval builds the society from the answers.

One plan for everyone: ₹10 per flat per month plus GST, a 30-day free trial, no ads and no paid resident tier. The landing page loads only a few kilobytes of text first and the fonts were cut by half, so a first visit on a mobile connection is quick.

// multi-society SaaS

Many societies, one installation

  • Data kept apart in the database

    PostgreSQL row-level security with composite tenant keys and forced RLS, and a test on every society table proving isolation.

  • Organisations

    Builders, facility-management companies and security agencies manage many societies from one login.

  • 126 permissions

    Nine ready-made society roles plus organisation and platform roles. Top roles must use two-step sign-in.

  • Settings, not code

    100+ settings, feature flags for gradual releases, and a vault for provider credentials.

  • About 90 notifications

    Each in three languages, through the inbox, push, WhatsApp, SMS, email and voice.

  • Built for the DPDP Act 2023

    Recorded consent, a privacy centre to see and export data, an audit log, and guards who never receive phone numbers or documents.

What is NivasGate?

NivasGate is a multi-society SaaS for Indian housing societies, built and owned by Digital Web Weaver and live at nivasgate.com. It covers the gate, residents and tenancies, maintenance billing and collection, double-entry society accounts, notices, polls, complaints and SOS.

Is any visitor approved automatically?

No. Every visitor is a decision a resident makes. If the resident doesn't answer the app, the request escalates to WhatsApp and then a phone call, and the guard sees each step.

How is each society's data kept separate?

By PostgreSQL row-level security with composite tenant keys, not only by application code, and every table that holds society data has a test proving one society can't see another's.

Does payment money pass through Digital Web Weaver?

No. Each society uses its own payment-gateway keys, so maintenance payments settle directly to the society's own bank account.
