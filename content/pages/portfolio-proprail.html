In short

PropRail is cloud property management and accounting software that Digital Web Weaver designed and built for a UK outsourced accounting firm running five property-owning SPVs and a management company. It replaces spreadsheets, separate property software and manual Sage entries with one system on one double-entry ledger: leases, UK quarter-day rent invoicing, rent reviews, RICS-compliant service charges, arrears, VAT returns and a Sage 50 UK export, across 9 user roles and 190+ automated tests.

// the problem — several companies, many tools, regulated service charges

What generic accounting and property software didn't solve together

  • Mixing up companies is a serious error

    Each SPV has its own owners, auditors, bank accounts and VAT registration. In spreadsheets, a receipt booked to the wrong company is easy to make and hard to spot.

  • Service charges are regulated

    The RICS professional statement on service charges in commercial property (2nd edition, mandatory from 31 December 2025) sets rules on budget timing, year-end accounts, the 18-month rule and fixed management fees.

  • Rent runs on UK quarter days

    Leases start and end mid-quarter, reviews are RPI-linked or open-market, and VAT depends on whether the owning company has opted to tax.

  • Work spread across tools

    Leases in one place, rent in another, service charges in spreadsheets and the books in Sage, with re-keying and reconciliation in between.

  • Very different people, different access

    Property managers, credit controllers, bookkeepers, senior accountants, SPV directors and external auditors each need a different slice of the data, per company.

// what we built — nothing edited after posting

From the lease to the VAT return and the Sage export

Every financial action posts through the same tested ledger, and a correction is always a traceable reversal. Screenshots show a demo portfolio; all companies, tenants, people and amounts in them are made up.

01 · Dashboards

A dashboard for every role

Nine roles, from Super Admin to Tenant and Auditor, each land on their own dashboard. The property manager sees lease expiries, rent reviews due and vacant units; the credit controller sees arrears by age band, a daily chase queue and recent payments; the SPV director sees occupancy, arrears and profit for their company.

A "Viewing entity" switcher at the top limits every screen to one company at a time.

  • 9 roles, access per company
  • Menus built from real permissions

02 · Analytics

Portfolio analytics with plain-English signals

For partners and senior accountants: annual rent roll, occupancy, total arrears, collection rate, service charge variance and open payables, each with a trend line. Charts cover rent collected against arrears, aged debt by band, occupancy by company and service charge budget against actual by category.

An "Insights & signals" panel flags risks in plain English, for example one tenant holding 74% of all arrears.

03 · Portfolio

Companies, properties and units

Each SPV and the management company has its company number, VAT registration, option-to-tax status, bank accounts and its own chart of accounts from a template. Properties carry the Land Registry title number, type and acquisition date, and every unit its floor area and let or vacant status.

Each property has a service charge schedule of cost categories and how each is shared. EPC ratings and statutory certificates (fire risk, gas safety, EICR, asbestos, buildings insurance) have expiry alerts and a compliance calendar.

  • Separate chart of accounts per company
  • Compliance certificates with expiry alerts

04 · Leasing

Tenants, leases and the event diary

Tenant records hold the company, contact, VAT number, deposit and guarantors, with tabs for leases, ledger and arrears. Leases record term dates, break clauses and notice periods, rent-free months, billing pattern, review type and service charge share; surrenders and mid-quarter starts and ends are prorated automatically.

The lease event diary puts rent reviews, breaks and expiries due in the next six months on one list, colour-coded by urgency.

05 · Rent

Quarterly rent invoicing and rent reviews

Pick an SPV and a UK quarter-day period and run the batch. Each lease is invoiced once per period, even if the batch runs again, and invoices go through draft and approval before posting. VAT is applied automatically: rent is exempt unless the owning company has opted to tax.

RPI-indexed and open-market rent reviews have a three-month look-ahead. Confirming a backdated review raises the shortfall invoice automatically.

  • Never invoiced twice
  • Option-to-tax VAT handled
  • Backdated review shortfalls invoiced

06 · Credit control

Tenant ledgers and arrears

Every tenant has a running-balance ledger and a printable statement. Receipts are allocated by hand or to the oldest debt automatically, and overpayments stay on the account as a credit.

The aged debt report sorts every open invoice into current, 30, 60 and 90+ day bands, filterable by company, property and band. First reminders for overdue invoices go out automatically, once per invoice, with a log of chase notes per tenant.

07 · Service charges

RICS-compliant service charges

An annual budget per property by cost category, shared by floor area, fixed share or lease share. The share of empty units is charged to the landlord, never spread onto the tenants who are there. A locked budget's on-account amounts flow into the next quarterly rent run.

Year-end reconciliation is done per tenant, time-apportioned for tenants who left mid-year, with a PDF certificate for each.

  • Budgets a month before the year starts
  • Year-end accounts within four months
  • 18-month rule and fixed fees flagged

08 · Payables & VAT

Suppliers, intercompany, VAT and Sage 50 UK

Supplier invoices are coded, approved and paid in payment runs; costs can be recharged to tenants with their own VAT treatment; and the management company's fee to each SPV posts on both sides automatically.

The quarterly VAT workbook compiles the nine return boxes per company, with an exception queue that blocks filing until VAT coding is fixed. An accountant compiles and a senior accountant signs off; filing locks the period. Each posted period exports to Sage 50 UK, mapped to the company's own nominal codes, and bank statements import by CSV.

  • Maker and checker on every return
  • Sage re-runs export only adjustments

09 · Reports

Reports that can never change the books

Rent roll, aged debt, budget against actual, profit and loss, balance sheet extract, cash position per company and the VAT summary, all exportable to CSV. Report screens are read-only, so they can never change posted figures.

10 · Phones

Works on a phone

Every screen is responsive, so a property manager can check the dashboard, properties or arrears from a phone. Companion apps for tenants and property managers (React Native and Expo) are in development on the same API and permission rules.

// security & data separation

Several legal entities, one system, no leaks

  • Separated inside the database

    PostgreSQL row-level security per company, with a second, fail-closed permission check in the application on top.

  • Roles per company

    The same person can manage one SPV and have no access to another. Access is checked on the server on every request.

  • Two-step sign-in

    Required for every financial and admin role. Users join by email invitation only; there is no public sign-up.

  • Append-only ledger and audit log

    Every posting and every sign-in attempt, with IP and time, is recorded and tied to a named user.

  • Safe uploads

    Lease and invoice documents are checked for type and size and stored outside the public web folder.

What is PropRail?

PropRail is cloud property management and accounting software for UK commercial property, built by Digital Web Weaver for a UK outsourced accounting firm. It covers leases, quarterly rent invoicing, rent reviews, RICS-compliant service charges, tenant ledgers and arrears, supplier invoices, intercompany recharges, VAT returns and a Sage 50 UK export, for several companies in one system.

Does PropRail work with Sage 50?

Yes. PropRail produces a correctly formatted Sage 50 UK batch file for each posted period, mapped to each company's own nominal codes. Re-runs export only the adjustments, never duplicates, so nothing is keyed in twice.

How does PropRail follow the RICS service charge rules?

Budgets must be issued at least one month before the year starts, year-end accounts are due within four months, costs older than 18 months are flagged, and management fees are fixed. PropRail checks each rule and flags it on screen, and the share of empty units is always charged to the landlord.

How is each company's data kept separate?

PostgreSQL row-level security separates every company's data inside the database, with a second, fail-closed permission check in the application. Roles are given per company, so a person can manage one SPV and have no access to another.

How was the accounting logic tested?

More than 190 automated tests cover every financial module and the full role matrix, and they run with static analysis on every change before deployment. Posted entries are never edited or deleted; a correction is always a traceable reversal.

What technology is PropRail built on?

Laravel 12 and PHP for the back end and the posting engine, Vue 3 with Inertia.js for the web app, PostgreSQL 16 with row-level security, and Redis with Laravel Horizon for batch jobs. Tenant and property manager mobile apps in React Native are in development.
