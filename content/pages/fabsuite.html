Do I need to install anything?

No — FabSuite runs in your browser, on a desktop in the office or a phone on the shop floor. Nothing to install, always the latest version, and your data stays safely in the cloud.

What if I already have my own cutting formulas?

That's exactly how it's built to work. You enter your own formulas per window type once, and calibration mode lets you check them against a real historical job before you rely on them.

Can my staff use it without seeing my margins?

Yes. Staff accounts can build and manage projects, leads, and quotes, but never see your rate card, margins, or billing — that's Owner-only.

What happens to a quote if my rates change later?

Nothing — a finalized project's numbers are frozen the moment you lock it. Editing your rate card later never retroactively changes a price you already quoted.

Is my data safe from other shops using FabSuite?

Yes — every shop gets its own fully isolated workspace. There's no cross-shop visibility, ever.

How is FabSuite different from other fabrication software?

Most fenestration software is sold as an annual licence, often installed on one computer. FabSuite is cloud-based and priced as a flat monthly subscription — no year-long commitment, nothing to install.

Is FabSuite built for small fabrication businesses?

Small shops first. One flat monthly price, no per-user fees, and no long contract. If your shop still quotes on paper or in Excel, FabSuite is built for you.
