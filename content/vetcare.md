1. **Appointments & Scheduling** — Calendar and list views per veterinarian, real-time availability preventing double-booking, public online booking link, live Kanban status board, automatic WhatsApp confirmation upon booking changes.
2. **Patient Records (EMR)** — Owner and pet profiles with photos, multiple pets per owner, allergy and chronic-condition flags, SOAP consultation notes with vitals, vaccination records with auto-calculated due dates, 24-hour edit lock, complete patient timeline.
3. **SARS-Compliant Billing** — Auto-generated invoices from consultations, proper 15% VAT calculation, sequential invoice numbers, SnapScan QR codes, payment tracking (cash, card, EFT, SnapScan), void functionality with audit trail, WhatsApp invoice delivery.
4. **WhatsApp-Native Communication** — WhatsApp gateway with SMS fallback, 10+ editable message templates, automated reminders, client segment broadcasting, message logs with delivery status, quiet hours enforcement (20:00–07:00), POPIA opt-out capability.
5. **Inventory & Stock Control** — Product catalogue with suppliers and batch numbers, FIFO dispensing enforcement, expiry tracking, auto-dispensing from consultations, low-stock alerts with reorder levels, expiry monitoring.
6. **Dashboards & Reports** — Role-aware dashboards (owners, vets, receptionists), live KPIs (daily revenue, active patients, low-stock count, outstanding balances), 6-month revenue trends, five exportable business reports in PDF and Excel formats.
