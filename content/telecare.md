## Core Features

1. **Secure Video Consultations** — High-quality, secure video calls between doctors and patients, with waiting room and queue management.
2. **E-Prescriptions** — Generate and share digital prescriptions instantly after a consultation, sent directly to the patient's app.
3. **Shared Patient Records** — Virtual and in-person visit records live in the same patient history — no information gaps between modes.
4. **Appointment Scheduling** — Patients book virtual or in-person consultations online with doctor-wise availability and reminders.
5. **Payments & Billing** — Collect consultation fees online before or after the call, with automatic invoicing and payment tracking.
6. **Branded Patient App** — Patients book, join consultations, view prescriptions and records, and get reminders — all from your branded app.
7. **Multi-Doctor Support** — Multi-doctor, multi-department configuration.
8. **Follow-up Reminders** — Automated reminders after consultations.

4. **Can it support multiple doctors and specialities?** Yes — TeleCare supports multi-doctor, multi-department setups with individual scheduling and consultation types.
