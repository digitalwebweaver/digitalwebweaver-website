## FAQ — BI Development FAQ
1. **What BI tool do you recommend?** — Metabase for self-service analytics with a business-friendly UI. Superset for more control. Power BI for Microsoft-integrated enterprises.
2. **How do you handle data quality?** — dbt tests validate row counts, uniqueness, referential integrity, and custom business rules at every pipeline run.
3. **Can you integrate our CRM and sales data?** — Yes — Salesforce, HubSpot, Pipedrive, and custom CRMs all have API or Fivetran connector support for warehouse ingestion.
4. **How long does a BI implementation take?** — Basic dashboard with one data source: 2–3 weeks. Full warehouse with multiple sources and automated reporting: 2–3 months.
