## FAQ Section
**H2:** "Questions we always get asked"

1. **Are these engineers full-time dedicated to us?** — Yes. When you hire through Digital Web Weaver, the engineer works exclusively on your project during the engagement hours. They're not split across multiple clients.
2. **What if the engineer isn't a good fit?** — We offer a 30-day risk-free trial. If the engineer isn't meeting expectations within the first month, we replace them at no additional cost. No questions asked.
3. **Who owns the code and intellectual property?** — You do, 100%. All code written during the engagement is your IP. Our contracts are explicit about this, and the NDA reinforces it from day one.
4. **What time zones do your engineers work in?** — We primarily operate on IST (UTC+5:30) but our engineers are flexible on 4 hours of daily overlap with your local time. For US East Coast and UK clients, this works seamlessly.
5. **What's the minimum engagement length?** — Our minimum is 1 month, though we find that engagements under 3 months rarely see the full benefit of onboarding investment. We recommend a 3-month minimum for best results.
