## Core Features

1. **Online Booking & Scheduling** — Clients book treatments and consultations online with real-time staff and room availability — no back-and-forth calls.
2. **Treatment Plans & Records** — Build multi-session treatment plans, log products used, and track progress with structured clinical notes.
3. **Before/After Photo Galleries** — Capture and organise progress photos securely against each client's record — great for results tracking and consultations.
4. **Digital Consent & Intake Forms** — Replace paper forms with digital consent, medical history, and intake forms clients fill before arriving.
5. **Billing, Packages & Memberships** — Sell treatment packages, memberships, and gift vouchers with integrated billing and automatic balance tracking.
6. **Automated Reminders & Follow-ups** — Cut no-shows with SMS/WhatsApp reminders, and bring clients back with automated post-treatment and re-booking nudges.

## Target Audience / Use Cases

- **Skin & Laser Clinics** — Track multi-session packages, device settings per session, and progress photos in one client timeline.
- **Med-spas & Wellness Centres** — Combine aesthetic treatments with spa services, memberships, and retail product sales.
- **Cosmetic Dental & Dermatology Practices** — Manage consultations, treatment plans, and recall schedules alongside billing and insurance notes.

## FAQs

1. **Can it handle multi-session treatment packages?** — Yes — build packages with a set number of sessions, track redemptions, and automatically prompt clients to book their next visit.
2. **Is client photo and medical data stored securely?** — Yes. Photos and health records are stored with access-controlled permissions, visible only to authorised staff against each client's record.
3. **Can clients book and pay online without calling the front desk?** — Yes — your branded booking page shows real-time availability, and clients can pay deposits or full amounts online during booking.
4. **Will it work across multiple clinic branches?** — Yes. Multi-branch clinics get centralised reporting with per-location scheduling, staff, and inventory.
5. **How soon can we be live and taking bookings?** — Most clinics are live within 4–5 weeks, including branding, data migration, and staff training.
