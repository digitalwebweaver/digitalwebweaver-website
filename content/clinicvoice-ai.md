## Core Features

1. **Automated Call Answering** — Responds to incoming calls instantly with a natural-sounding voice trained on clinic tone.
2. **Appointment Booking & Rescheduling** — Books and confirms appointments directly into calendars without human handoff.
3. **Automated Reminder Calls** — Places outbound reminder calls to reduce no-shows without staff involvement.
4. **FAQ & Information Handling** — Answers routine questions about timings, services, doctor availability, location, insurance.
5. **Post-Visit Follow-up Calls** — Places follow-up calls after appointments to check on patients.
6. **EMR & Calendar Integration** — Syncs with scheduling systems and patient records in real-time.
