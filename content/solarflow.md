## The Problem Section
Five pain points addressed:
1. **Subsidy Forfeiture (₹30k–₹78k)** — Non-compliant panels or net-metering rejection eliminates customer subsidies.
2. **Operational Chaos** — "Leads in group chats. Quotes in spreadsheets. Projects on paper registers."
3. **Quoting Delays** — Manual processes take "an afternoon" while competitors move faster.
4. **Lead Atrophy** — Insufficient follow-up causes prospects to sign with competitors.
5. **Silent Project Stalls** — No tracking of subsidy stage progression until customer complaints arrive.

## How It Works (Four-Stage Process)
1. **Bill Analysis** — "Snap the bill" via photo upload; AI extracts consumer data and recommends appropriate system capacity.
2. **Quote Generation** — Produces a branded PDF with BOM, PM Surya Ghar subsidy, net cost, and EMI options, plus compliance validation.
3. **Subsidy Tracking** — 6-stage state machine from GEDA approval to MGVCL net-metering to subsidy credited, with SLA timers.
4. **Lifecycle Service** — Underproduction Sentinel watches every system for performance issues; includes AMC and warranty management.

## The Subsidy Lifecycle (Six Stages)
1. PM Surya Ghar Registration
2. GEDA Approval
3. MGVCL Feasibility (with Net-Meter Pre-Check)
4. Installation & Commissioning
5. Net-Meter Inspection
6. Subsidy Credited

## Four AI Assistants
1. **Smart Quote** — Converts bill photos into a "subsidy-ready proposal in minutes"; extracts consumer number, sanctioned load, and units; builds priced BOMs with subsidy calculations.
2. **Net-Meter Pre-Check** — Validates the application against known MGVCL rejection reasons, including system sizing, ALMM compliance, inverter empanelment, documentation completeness, and earthing requirements.
3. **Underproduction Sentinel** — Continuous monitoring that "auto-raises a service ticket the moment a system dips" before customers notice performance issues.
4. **Follow-up Brain** — Scores leads by temperature, surfaces today's call list, and drafts WhatsApp nudges in English, Hindi, or Gujarati.

## FAQs
1. **Is data isolated per tenant/company?** Yes — multi-tenancy is confirmed with per-company data isolation.
2. **Is it compatible with DISCOMs other than MGVCL (e.g. DGVCL, PGVCL)?** Compatibility with MGVCL, DGVCL, PGVCL addressed.
3. **Can it be white-labelled/branded?** Yes — white-label/branding customization available.
4. **How is GST handled?** Auto CGST+SGST or IGST depending on the transaction.
5. **What do field technicians need?** Mobile-first, offline-capable field app on Android/iOS over 4G.
6. **Which AI provider is used?** Choice of Claude or OpenAI, using the customer's own API key.
7. **What is ALMM validation and why does it matter?** Ensures components ordered are on the government's Approved List of Models and Manufacturers, preventing subsidy-disqualifying purchases.
