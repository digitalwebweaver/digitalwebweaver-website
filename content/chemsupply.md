## Core Features

1. **Batch & Expiry Tracking** — Track every product by batch number, manufacturing and expiry dates, with full traceability from supplier to customer.
2. **Compliance Documentation** — Attach safety data sheets, certificates of analysis, and regulatory documents directly to products and batches.
3. **Order & Quotation Management** — Generate quotes and process orders with automatic compliance checks and document attachments.
4. **Distributor & Customer Portal** — Give distributors and bulk customers a branded portal to place orders, track shipments, and access documentation.
5. **Multi-Warehouse Inventory** — Manage stock across multiple warehouses with batch-level visibility and transfer workflows.
6. **Compliance & Sales Reporting** — Generate regulatory reports, expiry alerts, and sales analytics for management and audits.
